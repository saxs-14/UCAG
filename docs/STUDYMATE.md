# StudyMate -- AI Academic Support & Study System

StudyMate is an educational productivity system integrated into the **Uniwersiteit Companion / South African University Application Guidance (UCAG)** platform.

It empowers Grade 10-12 learners and university students to transition from high school to higher education by combining AI study planning, diagnostic mark evaluation, structured practice, full mock exam simulation, and Socratic tutoring.

---

## Key Capabilities

1. **Academic Diagnosis**: Analyzes current subject marks vs. target marks, identifies weak subject areas, and generates constructive study advice. Includes a clear non-medical disclaimer.
2. **Personalised Weekly Timetable**: Generates realistic weekly revision timetables allocating 60%+ of available study hours to weak subjects while incorporating rest breaks.
3. **Smart Study Plan**: Builds goal-driven 4-week revision plans with weekly topic milestones and actionable tasks.
4. **Study Material Organiser**: Categorises summaries, lecture notes, flashcards, and past papers by subject and topic.
5. **AI Quiz Generator**: Produces 5-10 question multiple-choice and short-answer quizzes with instant scoring and explanations. Verified by `assessmentValidator.ts`.
6. **AI Mock Exam Simulator**: 150-mark full matric paper simulator with a 3-hour timer, question navigation, text answer submissions, and official model answers.
7. **AI Socratic Tutor**: Interactive tutor following the *Explain → Example → Try → Feedback → Retry* learning method.
8. **Performance Tracking**: Visual mark progress bars, quiz score history, and weak area analytics.
9. **Past Papers & Memos**: Direct links to official DBE NSC past examination papers and marking guidelines.

---

## Provenance & Safety Rules

- **Non-Medical Disclaimer**: StudyMate does NOT diagnose learning disabilities, medical conditions, or cognitive impairments.
- **POPIA Privacy Shield**: Client-side document evaluation ensures no identity documents or personal data are stored externally without consent.
- **South African Curriculum Alignment**: Designed specifically for the NSC (National Senior Certificate) subject taxonomy (Mathematics, Physical Sciences, English HL, Life Sciences, etc.).
